## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are startup founders, operators, and ecosystem participants who are evaluating whether to learn more about or register for NTT Startup Challenge. They arrive with limited time and need to quickly understand what the event is, when it happens, and where to take action.

### Brand Personality
Credible, modern, and formal. The interface should feel confident and polished, closer to a premium event landing page than a playful startup marketing site.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark corporate event landing with strong NTT blue accents, `Plus Jakarta Sans` throughout, and a simplified hero inspired by the existing NTT Startup Challenge website. Use a light gray placeholder in the hero background for now. Do not use animation.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize immediate clarity: brand, event identity, and primary action should be obvious above the fold.
2. Keep the presentation polished and minimal: no unnecessary UI, no decorative motion, no feature creep.
3. Use contrast deliberately: dark surfaces, clear typography, and restrained blue accents.
4. Build for static delivery: all content should work cleanly as a statically generated site.
5. Treat SEO and semantics as first-class concerns from the start.
